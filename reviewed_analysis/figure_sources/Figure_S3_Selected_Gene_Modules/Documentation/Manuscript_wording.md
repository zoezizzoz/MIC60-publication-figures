## Figure S3 caption

Figure S3. Expression changes in selected genes associated with metabolism and genome regulation.
Panels show FOXO-associated genes (A), DNA-replication genes (B), spargel-associated genes (C), nine AMPK-associated genes selected from published Drosophila studies (D; see Methods), chromatin-regulation genes (E), and DNA-damage-checkpoint genes (F). Values are DESeq2 log₂ fold changes for female dMIC60-CS relative to dMIC60-WT, both in a dMIC60-null background (n = 3 independent biological libraries per genotype). Red and blue points meet both differential-expression thresholds (Benjamini–Hochberg-adjusted P < 0.05 and |log₂ fold change| ≥ 0.58); gray points have adjusted P values but fail one or both thresholds. Labels show adjusted P values below 0.05, including genes below the fold-change threshold. Point size represents −log₁₀(adjusted P), capped at 50. Open circles indicate unavailable adjusted P values; em dashes indicate unavailable fold-change estimates. These descriptive panels do not measure pathway activity. Gene-specific references and the full 131-gene AMPK homolog survey are provided in the supporting data.

## Results

Among the nine AMPK-associated genes selected from published fly studies (Fig. S3D), five had adjusted P values and none met both differential-expression thresholds; four lacked fold-change estimates or adjusted P values. The full 131-gene homolog survey is retained in the supporting data. These transcript-level results do not establish AMPK activity.

## Methods

Figure S3D shows nine genes selected for published experimental connections to AMPK in Drosophila: AMPKalpha, alc, SNF4Agamma and ACC (Pan and Hardie, 2002); Lkb1 (Castanieto et al., 2014); gig, S6k and Thor (Kim and Lee, 2015); and Atg1 (Ulgherait et al., 2014). This focused selection covers the AMPK complex, upstream regulation, metabolism, TOR-linked translation and autophagy. Selection used these biological roles rather than differential-expression thresholds. The panel is non-exhaustive and was assembled after the RNA-seq analysis. Gene-specific evidence and interpretation limits are recorded in AMPK_literature_selection.csv. Fold changes and genome-wide adjusted P values were taken unchanged from the existing female DESeq2 analysis.

The full survey of 131 fly homologs is retained in AMPK_grouped_gene_set_and_expression.csv. It was generated by mapping 122 human KEGG AMPK-pathway genes (hsa04152; accessed 24 September 2026; Kanehisa et al., 2025) using DIOPT 9.1 (Hu et al., 2011), retaining high- and moderate-confidence matches and deduplicating by FlyBase identifier. High confidence required best matches in both directions and a score ≥2; moderate confidence required a score ≥4 or a best match in either direction with a score ≥2. Membership, mapping scores and display-selection flags are provided with the supporting data.
