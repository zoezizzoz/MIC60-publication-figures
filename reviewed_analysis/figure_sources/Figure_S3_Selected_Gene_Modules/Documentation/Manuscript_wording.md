## Figure S3 caption

Figure S3. Expression changes in selected genes associated with metabolism and genome regulation.
Panels show FOXO-associated genes (A), DNA replication and genome maintenance (B), mitochondrial/metabolic functions (C), AMPK-associated genes (D), chromatin regulation (E), and DNA repair/checkpoints (F). The groups comprise genes with published functional or experimental associations, rather than validated direct-target sets. Values are DESeq2 log₂ fold changes for female dMIC60-CS relative to dMIC60-WT, both in a dMIC60-null background (n = 3 independent biological libraries per genotype). Red and blue points meet both differential-expression thresholds (Benjamini–Hochberg-adjusted P < 0.05 and |log₂ fold change| ≥ 0.58); gray points have adjusted P values but fail one or both thresholds. Labels show adjusted P < 0.05 regardless of the fold-change cutoff. Point size represents −log₁₀(adjusted P), capped at 50. Open circles indicate unavailable adjusted P values; em dashes indicate unavailable fold-change estimates. These descriptive panels do not measure pathway activity. Gene-specific evidence, original lists, display decisions and the full 131-gene AMPK homolog survey are provided in the supporting data.

## Results

Among the 11 AMPK-associated genes (Fig. S3D), seven had adjusted P values and none met both differential-expression thresholds; four lacked fold-change estimates or adjusted P values. The full 131-gene homolog survey is retained in the supporting data. These transcript-level results do not establish AMPK activity.

## Methods

The original Figure S3 lists were reviewed gene by gene against published Drosophila experiments. The revised panels contain 10 FOXO-associated, 28 DNA-replication/genome-maintenance, seven mitochondrial/metabolic, 11 AMPK-associated, 15 chromatin-regulation and 10 DNA-repair/checkpoint entries. Association includes expression responses, genetic interactions and functional experiments; it does not imply direct transcriptional regulation. Panel C groups genes by mitochondrial/metabolic function and is not a validated Spargel-target set. Seven entries were omitted from the previous figure because the specific FOXO relationship (Lsp1alpha, Lsp1gamma, Gadd45 and dap) or exact-gene experimental replication evidence (DNAlig1, DNAlig3 and RfC3) was not verified in the checked publications. This does not establish that these genes lack relevant functions. The original lists, evidence, limitations and individual display decisions are retained in the supporting data.

The AMPK panel includes AMPKalpha, alc, SNF4Agamma and ACC (Pan and Hardie, 2002); Lkb1 (Castanieto et al., 2014); Sesn (Lee et al., 2010); gig, S6k and Thor (Kim and Lee, 2015); and Atg1 and Atg8a (Ulgherait et al., 2014). Sesn and Atg8a were restored from the original list after verifying their experimental AMPK connections. The selection covers the AMPK complex, upstream regulation, metabolism, TOR-linked translation and autophagy. It is non-exhaustive and was revised after the RNA-seq analysis using biological evidence rather than differential-expression thresholds. Fold changes and genome-wide adjusted P values were taken unchanged from the existing female DESeq2 analysis.

The full survey of 131 fly homologs is retained in AMPK_grouped_gene_set_and_expression.csv. It was generated by mapping 122 human KEGG AMPK-pathway genes (hsa04152; accessed 24 September 2026; Kanehisa et al., 2025) using DIOPT 9.1 (Hu et al., 2011), retaining high- and moderate-confidence matches and deduplicating by FlyBase identifier. High confidence required best matches in both directions and a score ≥2; moderate confidence required a score ≥4 or a best match in either direction with a score ≥2. Sesn and Atg8a were included using fly experimental evidence independently of this orthology survey; the other nine displayed AMPK genes occur in the survey. Full mappings and display-selection flags are provided with the supporting data.

Gene-specific sources are in Supporting_Data/S3_original_gene_audit.csv and AMPK_literature_selection.csv. Full references are in Documentation/S3_original_gene_audit.md and Supporting_Data/AMPK_literature_references.csv. These passages document the current figure and can be adapted to the manuscript.
