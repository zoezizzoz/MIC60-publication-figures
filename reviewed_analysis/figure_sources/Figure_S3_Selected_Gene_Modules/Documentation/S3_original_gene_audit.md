# Original Figure S3 genes: individual evidence audit

25 September 2026. **Audit of 89 original group–gene entries, representing 86 unique genes.** This audit records the original lists before the subsequent display revision. The original RNA-seq values are unchanged; the resulting display decisions are documented in `S3_original_gene_display_decisions.csv` and `Revision_2026-09-25.md`.

The original lists have considerable biological support, but the evidence does not justify treating every panel as a validated direct-target set. This audit documents present-day publication support for each original entry. It does not recover or invent the publications originally used to select the lists.

## Main findings

- The chromatin list has gene-specific experimental support across several chromatin mechanisms. It is not a single complex or pathway.
- Most replication entries have experimental support. DNAlig4 and PCNA2 favor “DNA replication and repair.” timeout has chromosome-stability evidence. DNAlig3, DNAlig1 and RfC3 require care about annotation versus locus-specific experimental support.
- The FOXO list is mixed. GstE1 is supported by expression measurements in two foxo mutants (Liu et al., Fig. 3A, checked visually). ImpL2, Atg6, Atg8a, Rbf and Cat need qualified association wording. The checked sources do not establish the named FOXO link for Lsp1alpha, Lsp1gamma, Gadd45 or dap.
- The spargel panel combines srl with mitochondrial/metabolic genes. Their biological functions are supported, but the other six cannot all be called validated Spargel targets from this audit. COX4 has been explicitly tested in a spargel-overexpression study, with context-dependent results.
- The original AMPK panel contains supported members, including Sesn and Atg8a. Their earlier omission from a smaller panel must not be interpreted as lack of a published basis. Atg13, Sirt1 and S6k have qualified or indirect support. Canonical AMPK links for FASN2, FASN3 and bmm were not verified.
- Blm and Ku80 support a broader checkpoint-panel heading such as “DNA repair and checkpoint control.” No replacement is implied by this labeling issue.

## How to interpret the audit

Searches used each original symbol and relevant aliases with the panel/regulator name, followed by primary-paper and gene-record checks. Primary abstracts, indexed full text and accessible full-text results were reviewed. Citation metadata were verified against Europe PMC. Evidence location and limitations are recorded for every entry. Group membership was not judged using the dMIC60 fold changes or P values.

“Not verified” means the checked sources did not establish the specific relationship. It does not mean that no such publication exists. Some primary supplements could not be retrieved, including the complete Alic et al. target spreadsheet. Therefore this is a documented literature audit, not an exhaustive exclusion of every possible relationship. Genome-wide target tables that were not inspected were not counted as gene-level validation.

All experimental contexts listed below are D. melanogaster unless otherwise stated. Evidence from mammalian orthologs, an inferred database pathway, or a similarly named paralog was not accepted as direct fly experimental evidence. The Rbf item is a brief published Research Notes report and is identified as such.

For all panels, RNA abundance does not establish kinase activity, transcription-factor activity, chromatin modification or pathway enrichment. These are selected-gene displays. A documented biological association does not establish that the original selection was prespecified or comprehensive.

| --- | ---: | --- |
| Context-dependent | 9 | Published evidence is indirect, tissue-specific, phenotypic or associative. The exact mechanism needs qualified wording. |
| Not verified | 12 | Biological function is documented, but the named FOXO, Spargel or canonical AMPK link was not verified in the sources checked. |
