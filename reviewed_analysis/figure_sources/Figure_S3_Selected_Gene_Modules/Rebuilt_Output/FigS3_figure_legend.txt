Figure S3. Expression changes in selected genes associated with metabolism and genome regulation.
Panels show FOXO-associated genes (A), DNA replication and genome maintenance (B), mitochondrial/metabolic functions (C), AMPK-associated genes (D), chromatin regulation (E), and DNA repair/checkpoints (F). The groups comprise genes with published functional or experimental associations, rather than validated direct-target sets. Values are DESeq2 log₂ fold changes for female dMIC60-CS relative to dMIC60-WT, both in a dMIC60-null background (n = 3 independent biological libraries per genotype). Red and blue points meet both differential-expression thresholds (Benjamini–Hochberg-adjusted P < 0.05 and |log₂ fold change| ≥ 0.58); gray points have adjusted P values but fail one or both thresholds. Labels show adjusted P < 0.05 regardless of the fold-change cutoff. Point size represents −log₁₀(adjusted P), capped at 50. Open circles indicate unavailable adjusted P values; em dashes indicate unavailable fold-change estimates. These descriptive panels do not measure pathway activity. Gene-specific evidence, original lists, display decisions and the full 131-gene AMPK homolog survey are provided in the supporting data.
