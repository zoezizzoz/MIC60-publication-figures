# Provenance and interpretation limits

- **Fig3G:** the historical CSV contains blank survival times. Its historical R code drops missing times, assumes observed deaths and applies within-group IQR filtering. These assumptions have not been established for the present native Illustrator curve. Neither the exact curve-source match nor its `p < 0.0001` is certified. The recovered inputs are clearly marked UNVERIFIED rather than relabeled as current plotted observations.
- **Fig2B/C and Illustrator assembly:** membership/STRING-edge tables and working generators were recovered, but exact final September 20–21 native composition and manual label positions cannot be certified. Scientific source tables are distinct from Illustrator layout edits.
- **Final native layout:** `provenance/final_layout_2026-09-23/` records the completed common-width Illustrator pass. Its scripts retain machine-specific paths and exact object/geometry requirements; the matching native baselines are not included. The provided layout records do not make repository-only rebuilds pixel-identical to the final assembled figures.
- **Fig2D:** archived normalized plotting values are frozen in `plotted_data/Fig2D/`. The reviewed generator uses normalized values from its DESeq2 run, which may differ slightly across R/package versions. A statistical refit is not a replacement for the frozen plotting values.
- **RNA-seq:** count matrices and differential-expression tables are downstream analysis inputs. FASTQ processing is outside this update. The public sequencing record is [GEO GSE346353](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE346353). Fresh DESeq2 checks preserved female 98 up/108 down and male 540 up/505 down at adjusted P <0.05 and |log2FC| ≥0.58, with 7,456/9,695 testable genes. The largest fresh female log2FC difference (about 2.107, CG14355) occurs in an untested gene with missing adjusted P; numerical differences are retained in the audit rather than concealed.
- **TEM and Fig3N:** dots are image-field means. Fly/specimen-to-field identifiers are unavailable, so fields must not be represented as independent biological replicates. Fig3N includes 7 WT/25 CS fields from 22,531 connected objects.
- **Fig4A:** the workbook supplies 64 measurements without cell/image/culture/experiment identifiers. The current graph is descriptive and does not restore unsupported legacy P annotations.
- **Fig4B:** the current graph pools all 78 normalized wells within genotype and dose and shows mean ± s.e.m. across wells. Each well is treated as a biological replicate per author direction. Two culture preparations contribute at 0/20/40 mM (9 wells/genotype); one contributes at 5/10 mM (6 wells/genotype). Preparation IDs remain available, and no inferential tests are added. The July 15 control is labeled DMSO; vehicle equivalence across dates is not established by the recovered workbooks.
- **FigS4A:** OR is the odds ratio for one-sided Fisher enrichment; pBH is BH-adjusted P across six curated sets. Its background is the curated testable genes, not the whole transcriptome. Not significant is not evidence of pathway absence.
- **FigS4C:** 20 image measurements come from two experiments. Retain the experiment identifiers; image count is not biological n.
- **Environment:** Table S1's existing JavaScript formatter requires `@oai/artifact-tool`. macOS Quartz and Arial are used by current Figure 4 exports. The entire dependency environment is documented but not fully locked by a cross-platform lockfile.

These limitations do not remove available data from the deposit. They explain exactly what the recovered sources support.
