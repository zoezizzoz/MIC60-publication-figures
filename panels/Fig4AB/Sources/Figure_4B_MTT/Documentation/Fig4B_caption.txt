(B) Cell viability assessed by MTT after 0-40 mM H₂O₂ for 30 min in MIC60-null HeLa cells expressing FLAG-tagged dMIC60-WT or dMIC60-CS. Blank-subtracted absorbance was normalized to the corresponding control mean within cell-culture preparation and genotype. Each point represents one well. Lines and error bars show the mean ± s.e.m. across all normalized wells within each genotype and dose. For each genotype, n = 9 wells at 0, 20 and 40 mM and n = 6 wells at 5 and 10 mM. P values are from two-sided Welch t-tests comparing pooled normalized wells between genotypes at each treated dose, without multiple-testing adjustment.
